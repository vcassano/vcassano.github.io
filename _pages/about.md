I am a Full-time Associate Professor in the [Department of Mathematics](https://www.mat.exa.unrc.edu.ar) at the [University of Río Cuarto](https://www.unrc.edu.ar/), and a Researcher (Adjunto) at [CONICET](https://www.conicet.gov.ar/).

I completed my PhD in 2015 under the supervision of Tom Maibaum at [McMaster University](https://www.eng.mcmaster.ca/cas), Canada. My thesis focused on Default Logic, a particular approach to Nonmonotonic Reasoning.

From 2015 to 2017, I worked as a Postdoctoral Fellow at the McMaster Centre for Software Certification ([McSCERT](https://www.mcscert.ca/)), Canada.

In 2017, with the support of a CONICET Postdoctoral Scholarship, I returned to Argentina and joined the Logics, Interaction and Intelligent Systems ([LIIS](https://liisgroup.github.io/)) group, led by [Carlos Areces](https://carlosareces.github.io). Since then, I have been working at the intersection of Logic and Computer Science, using tools from the world of Modal Logics.

In 2019, I joined the Department of Mathematics at the National University of Río Cuarto as a Full-time Professor (Adjunto), where I teach logic and programming courses for mathematics and computer science students. In 2026, I was promoted to Associate Professor (Asociado).

I have also been a Visiting Assistant Professor at the [Guangdong Technion Israel Institute of Technology](https://www.gtiit.edu.cn) in Shantou, China.

In 2021, I joined CONICET as a Researcher (Asistente), and in 2025 I was promoted to the next position (Adjunto).

My research interests include:

_Non-monotonic Reasoning. Default Logics. Deontic Logics. Algebraic Logic. Dynamic Logics. Epistemic Logics. Logics with Data. Tableaux Systems. Proof Theory._
